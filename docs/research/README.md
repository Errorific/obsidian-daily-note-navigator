# Research

Investigations and analysis supporting the Daily Note Navigator plugin's
development, arranged by topic.

Research docs are evergreen -- they reflect our current understanding. When
new findings change earlier conclusions, update the document. If the change
is a significant reversal worth calling out, a brief note explaining what
changed and why is appropriate, but don't maintain the full history of old
conclusions inline.

- [Existing periodic-note plugins](existing-plugins.md) -- survey of periodic-notes, obsidian-journal, daily-note-calendar and what each gets wrong.
- [Obsidian CLI extension API](cli-extension-api.md) -- how `Plugin.registerCliHandler` works (Obsidian 1.12.2+) and patterns from Templater / QuickAdd.
